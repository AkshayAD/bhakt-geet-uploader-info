Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

BhaktGeet is an owner-only publishing tool for the Bhakt Geet / Sacred Echoes Bhakti channel. Its Google authorization application is named Bhakti Lofi Automation. It is not a public upload service and does not request Google account access from visitors to this website.

Google data accessed and its purpose

  • Google Drive: read-only access is used to read a designated release queue, file metadata, and reviewed final video files. Although Google's read-only Drive permission is broader than one folder, the application restricts its workflow to the configured queue and ready-media folder. It does not edit or delete Drive files.
  • YouTube: separate upload and read-only permissions are used to confirm the owner's designated channel, upload an approved video privately with a future publication time, and inspect video information and recent uploads to confirm status or resolve an uncertain upload.
  • Operational records: release identifiers, file hashes, review/provenance references, planned publication times, returned video identifiers, and compact upload status support validation and duplicate prevention. Descriptions contain a release marker used for reconciliation.

Storage and access

Source videos and the release queue remain in the owner's Google Drive. Uploaded videos, their descriptions, and publication settings are held by YouTube. The Google Apps Script worker processes video bytes temporarily while hashing and uploading them.

The separately authorized YouTube offline credential is stored in restricted Apps Script Script Properties, alongside a separate release ledger. Script project editors can access those properties, so editor access must remain restricted to the owner and any explicitly trusted maintainers. Short-lived YouTube access tokens are held in execution memory and are not added to the release ledger or status output.

During local authorization and preproduction setup, OAuth client configuration, the authorization handoff, and an offline credential copy may be held in a private local secret directory controlled by the owner. They are not intended for public repositories, this website, Drive release files, chat, or logs. Temporary authorization handoff files are removed after the local flow finishes.

Transfers and other uses

At the owner's direction, approved video bytes and release metadata are transferred from Google Drive through Google Apps Script to YouTube. Google processes data under its applicable service policies. A scheduled video may become public on YouTube at the owner's selected time.

The tool does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, add tracking profiles, train machine-learning models with it, or disclose it to unrelated services. Access by a trusted maintainer, if the owner grants it, is limited to maintaining this workflow. These static information pages contain no forms, analytics code, advertising, or application cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process routine web-request information under its own policies; private Google credentials and release data are not part of the site.

Google API data and Limited Use

Google API information is used to deliver the owner-directed publishing and verification functions described here. The tool's intended use and any transfer of information received from Google APIs must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable. No Google verification, certification, or endorsement is claimed.

Retention, stopping access, and deletion

The owner controls retention of Drive media and YouTube uploads through those services. Release ledger entries are retained to prevent duplicate uploads, until the owner makes a reviewed cleanup decision. Credentials remain until the owner removes or replaces them, or Google invalidates access. Credentials can expire or be revoked; operation is not guaranteed indefinitely.

The owner can stop the Apps Script scheduled trigger and revoke the relevant app permissions in Google Account connections. With separate authorizations, review the relevant connections for both the primary account and the YouTube identity. The owner can then remove the offline credential from Script Properties and remove local credential copies.

Revoking access or stopping the worker does not delete existing Drive files, ledger records, or YouTube videos, and does not cancel publication schedules already stored by YouTube. The owner must review or remove those records and schedules directly in their respective services.

Contact and changes

For questions about this owner-operated tool, use the owner's public channel: Sacred Echoes Bhakti on YouTube. Material changes to data use will be reflected in this policy before the changed use begins, with renewed consent where required.
