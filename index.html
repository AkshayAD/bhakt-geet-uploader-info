Personal publishing automation

From reviewed video to a scheduled release.

BhaktGeet (Google authorization app: Bhakti Lofi Automation) is a private tool used by the owner of Sacred Echoes Bhakti to publish the owner's reviewed devotional videos from Google Drive to the owner's designated YouTube channel.

This website provides public information about that tool. It does not accept uploads, connect visitors' Google accounts, or offer a public publishing service.

What the tool does

  1. Reads a designated queue and final video files from the owner's Google Drive using read-only access.
  2. Checks release metadata, file hashes, review evidence, and the intended channel.
  3. Uploads an approved video privately to YouTube with an explicit future publication time, then records its status to help prevent duplicate uploads.

Separate permissions, one owner

The Google-hosted worker runs with the owner's Drive access and permission to make API requests and manage its scheduled trigger. A separately authorized YouTube-only offline credential allows uploads and read-only channel/video checks. Access is limited by the owner's consent and can be revoked.

The tool does not create new audio or decide whether a recording is licensed. The owner reviews the final video and remains responsible for rights, metadata, audience settings, and publication choices.
